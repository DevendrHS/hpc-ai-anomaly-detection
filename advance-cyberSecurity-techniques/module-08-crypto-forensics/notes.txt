Introduction to Blockchain & Crypto Forensics ----

Blockchain forensics is the process of examining blockchain transactions to trace cryptocurrency
 movements, identify suspicious activity, and investigate potential financial crimes.


 What Are AML and CTF?
These abbreviations are important for exams and compliance work.
AML — Anti-Money Laundering
Measures designed to prevent, detect, and report money laundering.
CTF — Counter-Terrorist Financing
Measures intended to prevent and detect the movement of funds used to finance terrorism.




Cryptocurrency Fundamentals


Digital Asset: An asset that exists in digital form.
Virtual Asset: A digital representation of value that can be digitally traded, transferred, or used for payment under the applicable definition.
Crypto Asset: A digital asset associated with cryptographic techniques and distributed ledger technology, depending on the applicable definition.

 Blockchain ek type ki DLT hai jisme data blocks cryptographic links se connected hote hain aur network ke
  consensus rules ke through ledger ki accepted state maintain ki jaati hai.



  Blockchain is a type of distributed ledger technology in which records are organized into cryptographically 
  linked blocks. Consensus mechanisms help network participants agree on the accepted ledger state. Public blockchains
   can provide transaction transparency and traceability, while distributed nodes can improve resilience. Because modifying
    an earlier block can invalidate subsequent links and require overcoming the network's consensus rules, altering accepted history is generally difficult, though not impossible in every circumstance.

                        The Internet Iceberg: More Than Meets the Eye

1. What is Surface Web?
Surface Web is the part of the internet that anyone can access easily using
 search engines like Google or Bing.


2. What is the Deep Web?
The Deep Web is the part of the internet that search engines like Google cannot normally 
find or display in their search results.

What is the Dark Web?
The Dark Web is a small part of the internet that is intentionally hidden and usually requires 
special software, such as Tor Browser, to access.


                  Gateways to Anonymity: Tor & I2P


 I2P is a privacy-focused network that uses garlic routing and encrypted tunnels to 
 support communication and services within its own network.                 

                 How does I2P work? (Step by Step)
Suppose Alice wants to send a message to Bob using I2P.
Step 1: Alice connects to the I2P network using I2P software.
Step 2: Her software establishes encrypted tunnels through participating I2P routers.
Step 3: Alice's message travels through the appropriate tunnels.
Step 4: I2P's routing and encryption mechanisms help protect the communication from outside observation.
Step 5: Bob receives the message through the relevant I2P communication paths.





The Fuel of Illicit Trade: Cryptocurrency


Illicit trade means buying or selling goods and services illegally.


What is Monero?
Monero is a cryptocurrency designed with privacy protections built into its transaction system.


Blockchain forensics is the process of examining blockchain data to investigate transactions, identify patterns, 
and potentially connect cryptocurrency activity to real-world entities.



Example: Bitcoin investigation
Suppose investigators are examining funds connected to a cryptocurrency scam.
They might:
1. Identify a Bitcoin address associated with the suspected activity.
2. Examine its transaction history.
3. Follow the movement of funds between addresses.
4. Look for transactions involving known cryptocurrency exchanges.
5. Compare the blockchain evidence with exchange records or other legally obtained evidence.
6. Develop and test hypotheses about the people or services involved.



Bitcoin transactions are publicly visible and can often be traced between addresses, although identifying
 the people behind those addresses requires additional evidence. Monero provides stronger built-in transaction privacy, 
 making public blockchain analysis more difficult. Blockchain forensics uses technical analysis and supporting evidence to investigate cryptocurrency activity.



 The Investigator's Compass: Legal & Ethical Considerations


 Jurisdiction is the legal authority of a country, court, or other authorized body to deal with a particular matter.

Authorization means having the required legal permission or authority to perform a particular action.


Important authorization terms
A. Warrant
A warrant is a formal legal authorization issued by a competent authority, usually a court or judge, permitting specified actions under applicable law.
For example, a search warrant may authorize investigators to search a particular location or seize specified evidence.
The exact requirements depend on the jurisdiction.
B. Undercover authorization
An undercover investigation involves an investigator concealing their official role or using an assumed identity to investigate suspected wrongdoing.
Undercover activities may require specific authorization, oversight, and compliance with applicable laws and policies.
For example, an authorized investigator might participate in an online environment to gather evidence about a suspected criminal operation.
The investigator cannot assume that every undercover action is legally permitted.
C. Probable cause
Probable cause is a legal standard used in certain jurisdictions, particularly the United States.

Entrapment generally refers to a situation in which law enforcement improperly induces or persuades someone to commit an offence they would not otherwise have committed.


LEA Collaboration & Case Handling---

What is Tor Browser?
Tor Browser is a web browser designed to improve privacy by routing internet traffic through multiple volunteer-operated relays in the Tor network.
Investigators may use it to access publicly available websites on the regular web or .onion services during an authorized investigation.


What is a controlled environment?
It is a computer setup designed to isolate investigation activities from everyday work and reduce security risks.



A virtual machine is a computer system running inside another computer using virtualization software.


Finding Needles in a Haystack: Dark Web Search Engines-------



Ahmia
Ahmia is a search engine that indexes publicly accessible websites on the Tor network. It also applies filters to exclude certain abusive content.



NotEvil
NotEvil is another search engine designed for finding websites on the Tor network. Its current website describes keyword searching across indexed .onion services.



| Tool | Main purpose | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Ahmia** | Search indexed Tor websites with content filtering. | Filtering and incomplete indexing mean some websites will not appear. |
| **NotEvil** | Search its index of Tor websites. | Coverage, accuracy, and filtering need verification. |
| **Haystak** | Alternative search and discovery of Tor websites. | Availability and index coverage may vary. |
| **Torch** | Discover indexed Tor websites through keyword searches. | Results may be outdated, misleading, or unsafe to visit. |

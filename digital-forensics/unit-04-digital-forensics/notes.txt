What is Digital Forensics?

 gathering evidence, from computing equipment and various
storage device and digital media, that can be presented in a court of law in a coherent and meaningful format.

Why do we need Digital Forensics?

1.legal proceeding
2.identify weakness
3.recovering data
4.identify attackers

Types of Digital Forensics?

disc forensics
network forensics
live forensics
mobile forensics


What is Digital Evidence?

any information of probative value that is either
 stored or transmitted in a digital form”


Characteristics of Digital Evidence or Rule of Digital Evidence---

The digital evidence must have some characteristics to be disclosed in the court of law

authentic
complete
admissable
believable
reliable
